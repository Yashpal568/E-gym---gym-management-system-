# Milestones and Exit Criteria

M0: repo structure, env validation, lint/type-check/test/CI baseline.
M1: Clerk verification, internal roles, tenant resolver, authorization, audit and cross-tenant tests.
M2: plan selection, UTR submission, Super Admin review, independent verification, approval/rejection and idempotency.
M3: resumable onboarding, profile, branch, plans, fee settings, attendance settings, notifications and checklist.
M4: members and membership lifecycle.
M5: ledger, partial/full payments, receipts and adjustments.
M6: attendance and override audit.
M7: in-app notifications, web push, email reminders, scheduler, retries and delivery logs.
M8: dashboards, reports and marketing CMS/SEO.
M9: security hardening, backups, accessibility, E2E and staging.
Phase 2: native member app after Phase 1 contracts/workflows are stable.

A milestone is complete only when checks are executed and documented. Do not claim unexecuted tests passed.
