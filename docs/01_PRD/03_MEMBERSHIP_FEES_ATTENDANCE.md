# Members, Memberships, Fees and Attendance

## Members and memberships
Searchable member records, contact details, status, plan, start/end dates, renewals, expiry history and activity. Validate plan entitlements and tenant/branch ownership server-side.

## Financial ledger
Support charges, full/partial payments, outstanding balances, payment method, receipts, discounts/adjustments and explicit refund/void workflows. Never silently edit/delete posted financial events. Use integer paise or an exact-decimal representation, never binary floating point.

Dashboard metrics distinguish billed, collected, outstanding, refunded, adjustments and net collections. Date ranges and calculations must reconcile to the ledger.

## Attendance
Fast manual check-in, duplicate check-in protection, history, reports and branch scope. Overrides for expired/suspended memberships require permission and an audit event.
